# Content Goals

Weekly and monthly posting targets. Drew tracks these in content-calendar and content-idea-engine.

## Weekly targets
- LinkedIn posts: **2** per week
- X/Twitter threads: **3** per week

## Monthly targets
- Blog posts: **1** per month

## Streak rules
- Streak increments when **both** LinkedIn AND X targets were met last week
- Missing the blog monthly target does NOT break the weekly streak
- Streak resets to 0 if either weekly target missed

## Notes
- Blog posts take longer — missing a week is fine
- Targets are aspirational, not punitive — Drew alerts, but never spam-alerts
- If a campaign or burst week changes the goal, edit this file rather than the skill
